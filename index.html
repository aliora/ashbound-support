About this policy

Ashbound, also known as Dash Peak, is published by Polat Kargılı (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the game, its advertising services and this website handle information. You can contact us at polatkargili@gmail.com.

Your data & game saves

You do not need an account to play. Your equipment, settings, progress and run records are saved locally on your device. The game does not send those saves to a server we operate and does not provide its own cloud-save service. Your device or operating system may separately back up app data, depending on your settings.

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. Advertising services may derive a general location from your IP address, as explained below.

Local saves and advertising data are different. Keeping game progress on your device does not mean advertising services collect no information.

Optional advertising

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads. Watching an ad can offer a revive, extra loot, a daily chest or a card reroll. You choose whether to watch. Advertising and consent services may communicate with Google when the game starts or prepares ads, before you choose to watch one.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform may process:

  • IP address and general location inferred from it.
  • Device or app identifiers. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) requires your App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Advertising and interaction information, such as ads displayed, video views, app launches and interactions.
  • Performance and diagnostic information, including crash logs.
  • Consent choices needed to apply your privacy preferences.

Google uses information to deliver and measure advertising, perform analytics, maintain its services and prevent fraud or abuse. Personalization and tracking depend on your permissions, consent, region and applicable settings. Refusing tracking does not stop every kind of advertising-related processing.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for more about its processing and privacy controls.

Your privacy choices

Tracking on iPhone & iPad

You can allow or deny tracking when asked. To review your choice, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device. Tracking permission is not required to play or to qualify for an available rewarded-ad bonus. Ad availability still depends on your connection, consent status and the advertising service.

Advertising consent

When required by the consent service, a Privacy options action appears in the game under Settings > Game. Use it to revisit your advertising consent. This action is shown when the service reports that it is required. You can also review Google’s advertising privacy controls.

Retention, deletion & requests

We do not hold a server-side copy of your game saves. Removing the game’s local app data removes local progress; device backups are managed separately through your operating system. Contact support before deleting progress if you need help with these steps.

Google retains information under its own policies. Removing the game does not automatically delete information already held by an advertising provider. Use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google about information it independently controls.

If you email us, we and our email provider process your email address, message and any attachments to respond. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve requests, keep necessary records or meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion. We do not use support emails for marketing.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent or complain to your local data-protection authority. Email polatkargili@gmail.com for requests about information we hold. Requests about information independently controlled by Google may need to be directed to Google. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Younger players

The game is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it. The game’s store age rating may differ by country.

International processing & security

Advertising, email and hosting providers may process information outside your country. Their policies describe their processing, retention practices and applicable safeguards. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

This website

This page does not add advertising, analytics, cookies, external fonts or a contact form. GitHub Pages hosts it. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links take you to services governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date above. Check this page for the current policy.
